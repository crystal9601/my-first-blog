Where each amount goes on the profit and loss

Book reimbursements to the expense they cover. They are never wages, never owner's draws or distributions, and never on the W-2.

Monthly reimbursement routine

  1. By the 5th: the owner submits last month's request with mileage log, receipts and home bills.
  2. By the 10th: bookkeeping reviews it against the plan and the business-use percentages.
  3. By the 15th: the business pays from the business account and books it by expense type.
  4. Expenses must be turned in within 60 days of paying them. Any advance or overpayment comes back within 120 days.
  5. Keep a signed written plan on file, effective before the first reimbursement.
