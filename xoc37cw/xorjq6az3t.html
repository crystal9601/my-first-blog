    LLC and business structure

    Set up right, kept right

    Available as an add-on

    LLC setup and business structure planning is not part of the standard monthly plan. You can add it any time, and we agree on the scope with you before any work starts. Here is how this work fits into your 24 months once it is added.

    Months 1 to 2

    Formation and foundation

    LLC or corporation filed with your state, EIN from the IRS, operating agreement or bylaws signed, and business bank account opened.

    We need from you: owner names and address, signatures, and your bank name once the account is open. Bring your state approval, IRS EIN letter and photo ID to the bank.

    Month 3

    Books and records

    Bookkeeping set up, accounts connected, and your personally paid business expenses sheet started.

    We need from you: bank and card access, and receipts for anything you paid personally.

    Every quarter

    Structure fit check

    At your strategy meeting, we check whether your setup still fits your income, your properties and your plans.

    We need from you: news of new properties, partners, products or big contracts.

    Every year

    Good standing and protection

    State annual report (in North Carolina, LLCs file by April 15 each year, and corporations file by the 15th day of the 4th month after their tax year ends), franchise tax, licenses, and an insurance and asset protection review.

    We need from you: forward state letters right away and confirm insurance renewals.

    Year 2

    Grow the structure

    An S corporation or C corporation check using your real numbers, a holding company or trust review, and a buy-sell agreement if you have business partners.

    We need from you: your goals for growth, family and exit.

    Year 1

    Months 1 to 2

    Inventory

    List what you have today: will, trust, power of attorney, healthcare directive, and who is named in each.

    We need from you: copies of any existing documents.

    Months 3 to 4

    Beneficiary check

    Confirm who is named on retirement accounts, life insurance and pension survivor elections, and that it matches your plan.

    We need from you: current beneficiary forms or account screenshots.

    Months 5 to 6

    Trust funding

    Make sure each asset is set up the right way for your plan. Some, like your home or business interests, may be retitled to your trust. Others, like retirement accounts and life insurance, usually pass by the beneficiaries you name. A trust that owns nothing cannot do its job.

    We need from you: deeds and account titles.

    Months 7 to 8

    Business continuity

    Decide who runs or inherits each business, and check your operating agreement for what happens if an owner dies or becomes disabled.

    We need from you: your wishes for each business and your operating agreements.

    Months 9 to 10

    Gifting plan

    Plan any yearly gifts to children or grandchildren, timed before December 31.

    We need from you: who you want to help and how much.

    Months 11 to 12

    Year-end review

    Update the plan for anything that changed: a move, a property, a new business, a marriage or a birth.

    We need from you: any life changes this year.

    Year 2

    Months 13 to 18

    Refresh and organize

    Repeat the beneficiary and trust funding checks, and gather every document into one secure place.

    Months 19 to 21

    Digital assets

    A list of online accounts and passwords, and who can access them.

    Months 22 to 24

    Family meeting (optional)

    Help your heirs know where everything is and who to call.

    We revisit your estate plan right away when you buy or sell property, open or close a business, marry or divorce, welcome a child, or receive a large windfall.
