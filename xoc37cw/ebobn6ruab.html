Entering it on the return and checking the state return

  1. Enter the asset on the depreciation (Form 4562) screen under the Schedule C activity: description, date placed in service, cost, business use %, and the vehicle details for listed property.
  2. Section 179: enter the 179 amount on the asset. Tax software applies the business income limit and carries any excess forward. Compare its 4562 line 13 carryover to this sheet.
  3. Bonus: bonus is the default for qualifying property. To use regular MACRS only, make the election out of the special depreciation allowance for that class of property (the election applies to the whole class for the year).
  4. State check: confirm the state return's depreciation adjustment matches the adjustment column here. For NC, that is D-400 Schedule S: the bonus and Section 179 add-back lines in Part A, and the 20% deduction lines in Part B in each later year.
  5. A Section 179 carryforward is shown as deducted the next year; the state treatment of the carryforward year is not modeled.
  6. Keep the PDF from this page with your records. States that use add-backs (NC included) expect records of the federal deduction, the add-back and the recovery schedule.
